## 1. Executive PRD Review

The finalized Product Requirements Document (PRD v1.9) establishes the baseline for employee administration, geofenced and selfie-based attendance verification, leave management, working-hour-based payroll, payslips, reporting, and auditability.

### 1.1 Core Confirmed Baseline Philosophy
The defining business rule of this product is that **salary is proportional to approved payable working hours**, calculated under a calendar-day monthly distribution:
1. **No Arbitrary Half-Day Reductions:** Partial-day work is paid based on actual approved minutes/hours; employees are not forced into an arbitrary half-day deduction.
2. **No Monetary Penalties for Late/Early Punches:** Arriving after 10:10 AM or departing before 5:50 PM triggers descriptive attendance classifications (`Late`, `Early Departure`), but **does not impose an independent monetary fine or secondary deduction**. Pay reflects only the approved payable working time.
3. **Calendar-Day Hourly Rate & Accumulated Earnings Precision:** Hourly rate is derived each payroll month from the employee's monthly salary reference divided by calendar days ($D_{\text{month}} \times 8\text{ hours}$):
   $$\text{Daily Rate} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}}}, \quad \text{Hourly Rate} = \frac{\text{Daily Rate}}{8} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 8}$$
   Six-decimal minute rate precision does not guarantee exact full-month reconciliation. To prevent truncation drift, gross earnings are evaluated directly from accumulated payable minutes before final monetary rounding: $\text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}} \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$. All payroll amounts maintain uniform 64-bit integer paise precision (`BIGINT`).
4. **Paid Lunch Policy (DEC-01 Amended):** Lunch from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM is **PAID**. Lunch is **not deducted** from employee salary. On scheduled working days:
   $$\text{Daily Payable Hours} = \text{Approved Actual Working Hours} + 1.0\text{ Paid Lunch Hour}$$
   $$\text{Daily Payable Minutes} = M_{\text{actual\_approved}} + 60\text{ Paid Lunch Minutes}$$
   - Confirmed Full Day Benchmark: 7.0h approved actual work + 1.0h paid lunch = **8.0 payable hours (480 minutes)**.
   - Confirmed Partial Day Benchmark: 5.0h approved actual work + 1.0h paid lunch = **6.0 payable hours (360 minutes)**.
   - Absence Lunch Policy: Zero lunch credit on absence is a proposed engineering design default pending formal PO confirmation (`PO-ABSENCE-LUNCH`).
   - Partial Attendance Qualification: Qualification rules for partial attendance remain an open decision (`PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY`).
5. **Paid Sundays:** Sundays are paid weekly offs. Employees receive an automatic **8.0-hour paid credit (480 minutes)** in the monthly calculation without attending office.
6. **Paid Recognized Public Holidays:** Recognized public holidays receive an automatic **8.0-hour paid credit (480 minutes)** in the monthly calculation without attending office.
7. **Sunday & Public Holiday Overlap:** Overlapping Sunday and public holiday receives exactly **8.0 paid hours (480 minutes)**, preventing duplicate credit while preserving dual calendar classification.
8. **DEC-15 Approved Option A (Effective-Date Proration):** Mid-period salary changes evaluate unrounded segment earnings with full internal precision, sum the unrounded segment earnings, and apply Half-Up rounding once to combined gross earnings.
9. **Normal Hourly Rate for Extended Hours:** Additional verified time worked beyond 6:00 PM is credited **only when approved by Admin** and is paid at the **normal hourly rate ($1.0\times$)** with no separate overtime premium or multiplier.
10. **Standard Office Attendance & Sales Team Field Exception:**
    - **Standard Office Flow (Mandatory for ALL Employees, including Sales Team at Office):** Requires office geofence validation ($\le 100\text{m}$ legacy baseline; **proposes tightening to $\le 50\text{m}$ pending formal Product Owner approval under `PO-ATT-GEOFENCE-50M`**), GPS horizontal accuracy threshold ($\le 30\text{m}$), mandatory live camera selfie, liveness detection, 1:1 facial match against Admin-approved enrollment reference, and spoofing safeguards. Up to 3 automatic verification attempts are permitted before routing to `Verification Pending` for Admin review; outside-geofence standard punches are rejected and poor accuracy triggers retry.
    - **Sales Team Field Attendance Exception (`PO-ATT-FIELD-SALES` — Proposed Architecture Revision Pending PO Sign-Off):** A separate exception-only workflow available exclusively to employees assigned to the Sales Team when performing work at distant customer/sales sites unable to reach the office (hidden from and blocked for normal employees). Enforces mandatory live selfie, face match, liveness, actual GPS coordinates + accuracy ($\le 30\text{m}$), and mandatory activity/location comment without enforcing the 50m office geofence; enters strictly `PENDING_REVIEW` (never auto-approved), requires Admin `APPROVE` or `REJECT`, preserves DEC-02 single daily punch pairs without overwriting raw events, and never independently alters payable hours or payroll math.
11. **Admin-Only Missing Punches:** Employees cannot create missing timestamps. Missing check-ins or check-outs can be inserted only by an Admin with a mandatory audit reason.
12. **Dual-Key Payroll Approval:** Admin prepares and submits the monthly payroll batch; Super Admin exercises exclusive approval or rejection authority. Payslips are generated only post-approval.
13. **Actual Working-Time Measurement Technical Blocker (`BLOCKER-ACTUAL-WORK-MEASUREMENT`):** A single check-in/check-out pair captures elapsed attendance span, not granular productive working intervals. The system must not equate elapsed presence with productive work, nor assume computer inactivity is non-working time. An auditable measurement or approval mechanism must be designed before Phase 4.
14. **Deployment Scope:** Initial deployment targets 1 organization, 1 office, and approximately 10 employees. The architecture must support multi-office growth without requiring an initial microservices architecture.

The proposed engineering design default is 0m credit ($M_{\text{actual\_approved}} = 0, M_{\text{lunch\_credit}} = 0$), pending formal PO confirmation. Keeps full absence, partial-day qualification (`UNASSIGNED-PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY`), leave pay treatment (`UNASSIGNED-PO-LEAVE-PAY`), and holiday spans (`UNASSIGNED-PO-HOLIDAY-LEAVE`) strictly separate.
  - **Confirmed Full Day Benchmark:** 7h actual + 1h lunch = **8.0 payable hours (480 mins)** = Daily Rate.
  - **Confirmed Partial Day Benchmark:** 5h actual + 1h lunch = **6.0 payable hours (360 mins)**.
  - **Partial Attendance Lunch Policy (UNASSIGNED-PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY Open):** Qualification criteria for crediting 60m paid lunch during partial attendance (works only before lunch, works only after lunch, works across lunch, partial-day leave, minimum actual hours threshold) remain open Product Owner decisions.

- **Calendar-Day Monthly Salary Reference & Exact Accumulated Earnings:** Monthly salary reference is distributed across calendar days of the payroll month ($D_{\text{month}} \in \{28, 29, 30, 31\}$):
  $$\text{Daily Rate} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}}}, \quad \text{Hourly Rate} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 8}$$
  Six-decimal minute rate precision does not guarantee zero truncation loss. To prevent drift, gross earnings are evaluated directly from accumulated payable minutes before final monetary rounding:
  $$\text{Gross Earned (Paise)} = \text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}} \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$$
  Monetary amounts (gross, allowances, adjustments, deductions, net) maintain 64-bit integer paise precision (`BIGINT`). Derived rates (`daily_rate`, `hourly_rate`, `minute_rate`) may be stored as `NUMERIC(14,6)` for fractional display and reporting, but `NUMERIC(14,6)` is fractional decimal precision, not exact rational arithmetic, and must not be used as the authoritative calculation source. Authoritative gross earnings use exact rational numerator and denominator arithmetic: $\text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left(\frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}} \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480}\right)$. Whole-rupee rounding of net pay is an open Product Owner decision (`DEC-05: Net Salary Rounding Rule`). Reconciles mathematically to exact reference salary $S_{\text{ref}}$ across 28, 29, 30, and 31-day months.
- **Confirmed DEC-15 Option A (Effective-Date Salary Proration):** Mid-month salary revisions apply the salary reference active on each specific calendar date. Unrounded segment earnings are evaluated with full internal precision, summed, and rounded once to integer paise using Half-Up rounding.
- **Actual Working-Time Measurement Technical Blocker (`BLOCKER-ACTUAL-WORK-MEASUREMENT`):** A single check-in/check-out pair captures elapsed attendance span, not granular productive working intervals. Productive working time is not currently measurable. The system must not equate elapsed presence with productive work, nor assume computer inactivity is non-working time. An auditable measurement or approval mechanism must be designed and approved before Phase 4. Illustrative alternatives (task logging, desktop monitoring, supervisor timesheet approval) represent possible approaches requiring separate evaluation and explicit approval; no surveillance/monitoring method is recommended as an approved architecture.
- **Confirmed Daily Attendance Session Rule (DEC-02 Approved Option A — Maximum One Pair):**
  - **Maximum one CHECK_IN** and **maximum one CHECK_OUT** event per employee per scheduled working day ($\le 1$ of each).
  - A completed attendance record requires one valid CHECK_IN and one valid CHECK_OUT.
  - Absence, approved leave, and incomplete attendance (unpaired punches) are **valid business states**; the system never forces a punch event.
  - No employee-initiated second check-in or second check-out is permitted on the same working day. Multiple attendance sessions or split shifts are **strictly unsupported in Phase 1 or Phase 2**.
  - **Check-Out Validation Order:** (1) If CHECK_OUT already exists $\rightarrow$ `ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_COMPLETED`; (2) Otherwise, if CHECK_IN does not exist $\rightarrow$ `CHECK_OUT_WITHOUT_CHECK_IN`; (3) Otherwise, process check-out.
  - **Unpaired Check-Out Exception Policy:** Normal employee API check-out without a valid check-in is strictly rejected. An unpaired check-out may arise only through exceptional administrative manual correction, legacy data import, or data-integrity issues; all such records are flagged `requires_admin_review = true` and generate 0 payable minutes until resolved by an Admin. No new employee-facing attendance workflow is introduced.
  - **Admin Correction Distinction:**
    - *Workflow A (Missing Punch):* Admin creates the missing event in `attendance_events` (`is_manual_entry = true`), recording actual timestamp and mandatory reason.
    - *Workflow B (Incorrect Existing Punch):* Admin stores the corrected effective timestamp in `attendance_corrections` and updates `attendance_days`, preserving the raw event and maintaining `UNIQUE(attendance_day_id, event_type)`.
  - **Idempotency Semantics:** Same `Idempotency-Key` + identical payload returns the original stored response without creating a new event (HTTP 200/201 replay); different key + duplicate attendance action returns HTTP 409 Conflict; same key + different payload returns HTTP 422 with `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`. DB constraints provide atomic protection against concurrency race conditions.
- Arrival grace: 10 minutes (arrival after 10:10 AM is labeled `Late`).
- Departure grace: 10 minutes (departure before 5:50 PM is labeled `Early Departure`).
- Overtime: Time beyond 6:00 PM counted only when approved by Admin, paid at flat $1.0\times$ normal hourly rate.
- Office geofence radius: `100m` legacy baseline (**proposed tightening to `50m` pending formal Product Owner approval under `PO-ATT-GEOFENCE-50M`**); maximum acceptable GPS horizontal accuracy threshold remains $\le 30$ meters. Applies to all employees (including Sales Team when at office).

Outside-office-geofence standard punches are rejected; poor GPS accuracy prompts retry; suspected GPS spoofing is rejected or flagged under security controls.

### 2.2 Recommended Technical Decisions
- **Tenant-Aware Foundation & Automated Isolation Testing:** Include `organization_id` on all core database tables, derive tenant context server-side (`SET LOCAL app.current_org_id` in explicit transaction under `NOBYPASSRLS` role), enforce closed-ring composite FKs, and verify isolation with automated integration suite (`SUITE-TENANT-ISOLATION`).
- **Strict Financial Precision:** Model all monetary reference values, earned salaries, allowances, adjustments, and net pay as 64-bit integer paise (`BIGINT`), completely eliminating floating-point rounding errors.
- **Standardized Rate Precision & Accumulated Earnings:** Derived daily, hourly, and minute rates may be stored as `NUMERIC(14,6)` for fractional display and payslip reporting. However, `NUMERIC(14,6)` is fractional decimal precision, not exact rational arithmetic, and must not be used as the authoritative calculation source. Authoritative gross earnings are evaluated directly using exact rational numerator and denominator arithmetic: $\text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left(\frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}} \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480}\right)$. Unrounded segment earnings are summed across DEC-15 segments and rounded once to integer paise, guaranteeing zero truncation drift.
- **Hexagonal Biometric Adapter:** Implement an `IFaceVerificationProvider` abstraction interface to decouple the domain application from external biometric vendors, allowing local mock implementations during Phase 1–2 development.
- **Native Supabase Storage Pre-Signed URLs:** Leverage Supabase Storage's native signed URL generator (`createSignedUrl` with 15-minute maximum TTL) for private evidence access rather than custom HMAC infrastructure.
- **Express Authentication Broker:** Express backend coordinates authentication, translating employee phone numbers to an internal format in Supabase Auth to enable phone + password authentication without SMS OTP overhead.
- **Immutable Append-Only Audit Trail:** Utilize PostgreSQL trigger `trg_audit_logs_no_mutation` blocking `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on `audit_logs` across all 8 operational domains, capturing `payload_before`, `payload_after`, actor, action, timestamp, and justification reason.
- **Multi-Tiered Backup & Automated DR Testing:** Continuous WAL streaming for PITR ($RPO \le 1\text{ hour}$, actual $\le 5\text{ mins}$), daily automated base snapshots (30-day retention), weekly cold archives (90 days / 1 year), $RTO \le 4\text{ hours}$, and monthly automated CI/CD sandbox restore rehearsals (`SUITE-DR`).

### 3.2 Daily Attendance Session Architecture & Admin Correction Policy (DEC-02 Approved Baseline)
- **Approved Policy Status:**
  The Product Owner officially **APPROVED DEC-02 (Option A: One Check-In and One Check-Out Pair Per Employee Per Working Day)** on 2026-11-07. This rule is now an active, confirmed business requirement incorporated into PRD v1.5.
- **Confirmed Attendance Session Rules (Maximum One Pair):**
  1. Each employee is restricted to a **maximum of one CHECK_IN** and a **maximum of one CHECK_OUT** event per scheduled working day ($\le 1$ of each).
  2. A completed attendance record requires exactly one valid CHECK_IN and one valid CHECK_OUT.
  3. **Absence, approved leave (CL/SL/PL/LWP), and incomplete attendance (unpaired punch) remain valid business states.** The system must never force a punch event merely to satisfy an attendance constraint.
  4. Each employee has at most one attendance working interval per day. No employee-initiated second check-in or second check-out is permitted on the same working day.
  5. Multiple attendance sessions, split shifts, or pause/resume intervals are **strictly unsupported** in Phase 1 and Phase 2.
- **Deterministic Check-Out Validation Order:**
  When evaluating a check-out request, the backend evaluates rules in this exact deterministic order:
  1. **If CHECK_OUT already exists:** Immediately reject with HTTP 409 Conflict (`ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_COMPLETED`).
  2. **Otherwise, if CHECK_IN does not exist:** Immediately reject with HTTP 409 Conflict (`CHECK_OUT_WITHOUT_CHECK_IN`).
  3. **Otherwise:** Process the check-out event.
- **Check-In Validation & Error Handling:**
  - If a CHECK_IN already exists for that calendar day, reject with HTTP 409 Conflict (`ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`).
  - *No Employee Self-Service Corrections:* Employees cannot independently correct, replace, overwrite, or delete an attendance record.
  - *Incomplete Attendance Review:* Any day with an unpaired punch (e.g., forgot to check out) is flagged `requires_admin_review = true` and contributes 0 payable minutes until reviewed and completed by an Admin.
- **Precise Idempotency & Concurrency Semantics:**
  - *Same `Idempotency-Key` + identical request payload:* Returns the original stored response without creating a new event (HTTP 200/201 replay).
  - *Different `Idempotency-Key` + duplicate attendance action:* Rejection with HTTP 409 Conflict (`ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN` or `ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_COMPLETED`).
  - *Same `Idempotency-Key` + different payload:* Rejection with HTTP 422 Unprocessable Entity (`IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`).
  - *Database Uniqueness Guarantee:* The database constraint `UNIQUE(attendance_day_id, event_type)` serves as the final atomic barrier against race conditions and concurrent double-taps.
- **Admin Correction Policy (Two Explicit Workflows):**
  All attendance corrections must be performed exclusively by an authorized Admin (or Super Admin).
  
  **Workflow A — Missing Punch (No prior punch of that type existed):**
  - Admin manually creates the missing attendance event (`CHECK_IN` or `CHECK_OUT`).
  - The event is created in `attendance_events` with `is_manual_entry = true`.
  - Records actual reported timestamp, mandatory reason ($\ge 10\text{ chars}$), acting Admin ID, and server timestamp.
  - Satisfies `UNIQUE(attendance_day_id, event_type)` because no prior row existed for that event type.
  - Preserves all original evidence and calculates the working interval.

  **Workflow B — Incorrect Existing Punch (A raw event already exists):**
  - **Never create a duplicate event:** The system must never create a secondary `CHECK_IN` or `CHECK_OUT` in `attendance_events` (which would violate `UNIQUE(attendance_day_id, event_type)`).
  - **Never overwrite original raw event:** The original raw event, GPS coordinates, selfies, and verification telemetry in `attendance_events` and `attendance_evidence` remain 100% unaltered.
  - **Record in Correction History:** The corrected effective timestamp is recorded in `attendance_corrections` along with `previous_state` and `updated_state` JSON snapshots, acting Admin user ID, server timestamp, and mandatory explanation note.
  - **Effective Timestamp in Daily Record:** The effective timestamp is updated on `attendance_days` (`first_check_in_at` or `last_check_out_at`), ensuring that payable minute calculations, Amended DEC-01 paid lunch crediting, and payroll calculations use the approved effective timestamp.
- **Compatibility with Amended DEC-01 Paid Lunch Policy:**
  Because each employee has at most one attendance working interval per day, the 60-minute paid lunch credit is applied **strictly once per day** to that single valid interval:
  $$\text{Daily Payable Minutes} = M_{\text{actual\_approved}} + 60\text{ Paid Lunch Minutes}$$
  There is zero risk of double lunch crediting or multi-session calculation conflicts.

### 3.4 Security Architecture: Reassessment of Client-Side Spoofing & Auth Design

#### 3.3.1 Critical Reassessment of Browser GPS Spoofing Controls
The PRD requires GPS geofencing (`100m` legacy baseline / **`50m` proposed under `PO-ATT-GEOFENCE-50M` pending formal approval**) and accuracy checks ($\le 30\text{m}$). The technical limitations of browser-based web applications and PWAs must be explicitly recognized:
1. **No Low-Level OS Mock Location Detection in Web APIs:** The standard HTML5 Geolocation API (`navigator.geolocation`) runs in browser sandboxes (Safari, Chrome) and does not expose native Android `isFromMockProvider` or iOS security flags to client JavaScript.
2. **IP Geolocation Imprecision:** Cellular IP addresses resolve to mobile network carrier gateway GGSN/PGW nodes, often located tens or hundreds of kilometers away from the actual user. Cross-referencing GPS against IP geolocation cannot reliably validate a 50-meter or 100-meter office geofence and will produce false positives.
3. **EXIF Metadata Limitations:** In browser web apps utilizing `MediaDevices.getUserMedia`, video frames captured via `<canvas>` do not contain raw camera EXIF metadata unless written by the application itself.
4. **Realistic Security Posture:**
   - Client-side checks **do not reliably prevent GPS spoofing** on web/PWA platforms.
   - The application enforces server-side controls: immutable server timestamps, strict Haversine distance calculation using authoritative server-stored office coordinates, $\le 30\text{m}$ accuracy gate, impossible-travel / velocity anomaly detection (rejecting or flagging suspected spoofing), and rate-limiting punch attempts. For Sales Team Field Attendance Requests (`PO-ATT-FIELD-SALES`), actual outside-office GPS coordinates and computed distance are retained truthfully (`is_within_office_geofence = FALSE`) and never falsified.
   - GPS proximity proves only that a device reporting those coordinates was near the perimeter (or at the reported field site); true physical presence relies on the secondary verification layer (facial match + liveness) and, for field exceptions, mandatory Admin review.
   - If strict hardware-level anti-spoofing is required, native mobile apps (Android/iOS) with device integrity attestations (Google Play Integrity / Apple DeviceCheck) must be considered in a future phase.

### 3.6 Standardized Financial Precision Model
To prevent rounding drift across daily ledgers, monthly batches, and payslips, financial precision is standardized across all documentation, database DDL, and calculation modules:

1. **Monetary Amounts (Integer-Paise BIGINT):** Base monthly reference salary, gross earned salary, allowances, deductions, approved adjustments, and final net payable salary are whole-currency amounts modeled and stored as 64-bit integer paise (`BIGINT`), with non-negative check constraints where appropriate.
2. **Scheduled Hours & Minutes (Calendar-Day Distribution):**
   - Standard scheduled hours ($H_{\text{std}}$) = $D_{\text{month}} \times 8.0$ hours (where $D_{\text{month}} \in \{28, 29, 30, 31\}$).
   - Standard scheduled minutes ($M_{\text{std}}$) = $D_{\text{month}} \times 480$ minutes.
3. **Derived Rates for Reporting & Authoritative Arithmetic:**
   - Derived rates do **not** necessarily evaluate to whole paise (e.g. ₹20,000 / 30 yields 66,666.666667 paise/day, 8,333.333333 paise/hr, 138.888889 paise/min) and must **never** be forced into integer `BIGINT` columns.
   - Derived daily, hourly, and minute rates may be stored as **`NUMERIC(14, 6)`** for fractional display and payslip reporting. However, `NUMERIC(14, 6)` is fractional decimal precision, **not exact rational arithmetic**, and must **never** be used as the authoritative calculation source for accumulated earnings.
   - Authoritative gross earnings calculation evaluates exact rational numerator and denominator arithmetic:
     $$\text{segment\_earnings\_paise}_k = \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}, k} \times S_{\text{ref}, k, \text{paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480}$$
   - Unrounded segment earnings are summed across DEC-15 effective-date segments with full rational precision, and rounded once at the gross level using Half-Up rounding. Intermediate segment-level rounding is strictly prohibited.
4. **Daily Calculation:** Net payable minutes ($M_{\text{payable}}$) aggregated at minute-level resolution: $M_{\text{payable}} = \text{Approved Actual Work Minutes} + \text{Paid Lunch Minutes (60m on qualifying attendance)} + \text{Automatic Credit Minutes (480m on Sunday/Holiday)}$. Full-day absence awards 0m lunch credit as a proposed engineering design default (`UNASSIGNED-PO-ABSENCE-LUNCH` pending PO confirmation), with partial-day qualification governed by open decision `UNASSIGNED-PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY`.
5. **Monthly Gross Earned Salary (Accumulated Single Rounding):**
   - For a single continuous monthly salary:
     $$\text{Earned Salary (paise)} = \text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}} \times \text{Monthly Reference Salary (paise)}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$$
   - For mid-period salary revisions (DEC-15 Option A): Each salary segment is evaluated with full unrounded rational precision, summed across all segments, and rounded once:
     $$\text{Earned Salary (paise)} = \text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \sum_{k} \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}, k} \times S_{\text{ref}, k, \text{paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$$
     *(Segment-level intermediate rounding is strictly prohibited to avoid multi-paise drift).*
6. **Net Salary Compilation:**
   $$\text{Net Salary (paise)} = \text{Earned Salary (paise)} + \sum \text{Allowances (paise)} + \sum \text{Adjustments (paise)} - \sum \text{Deductions (paise)}$$
7. **Payslip & Disbursement Precision (Zero Rupee Rounding):** Net payable salary is retained, stored, and disbursed in exact 64-bit integer paise (`BIGINT`). Mandatory whole-rupee rounding has been eliminated. The introduction of any whole-rupee display or settlement rounding is formally registered as open decision **`DEC-05`** (`Net Salary Rounding Rule`) pending explicit Product Owner approval.
8. **Calendar-Day Denominator Referential Integrity & Tenant Safety Guarantee:** Standard SQL `CHECK` constraints are row-local and cannot evaluate values across tables. In relational theory, snapshotting `calendar_days` onto `payroll_runs` and `payroll_items` represents intentional controlled denormalization (Financial Snapshot Pattern) to ensure financial ledger immutability and standalone auditability without dynamic join dependency. To prevent denominator corruption, update anomalies, or cross-tenant leakage from ad-hoc database maintenance scripts, data fixes, or application bypasses, referential integrity and tenant scoping are enforced via chained declarative composite foreign keys:
   - `payroll_periods`: Enforces intra-row `CHECK (calendar_days = (end_date - start_date + 1))` and exports candidate superkey `UNIQUE (organization_id, id, calendar_days)`.
   - `payroll_runs`: Inherits `organization_id` and `calendar_days INTEGER NOT NULL` with `FOREIGN KEY (organization_id, payroll_period_id, calendar_days) REFERENCES payroll_periods(organization_id, id, calendar_days) ON DELETE RESTRICT` and exports candidate superkey `UNIQUE (organization_id, id, calendar_days)`.
   - `payroll_items`: Inherits `organization_id` and `calendar_days INTEGER NOT NULL` with `FOREIGN KEY (organization_id, payroll_run_id, calendar_days) REFERENCES payroll_runs(organization_id, id, calendar_days) ON DELETE RESTRICT`.
   This guarantees at the PostgreSQL engine level in a single atomic tuple check that no line item can ever reference a mismatched denominator or belong to a different tenant.

  3. **Comprehensive Multi-Tier Deletion Coverage:** Deletion executed by the automated purge worker covers:

  6. **Strict Decoupling from Differentiated Statutory Records:** Deleting a selfie does not delete or alter the underlying attendance record. Punch timestamps, net working minutes, verification confidence scores, and payroll items remain intact. Rather than assuming a universal 3–5 year retention period, records are governed by separate statutory categories:
     - *Daily Attendance Selfies (Tier 2):* Maximum 30 calendar days (auto-purge).
     - *Employment & Attendance Registers:* 3 years under Telangana Shops & Establishments Act 1988 §62 (Rules Form XXII). *[Pending legal counsel verification]*.

     - *Audit Logs:* At least 3 years immutable append-only. *[Pending legal counsel verification]*.
  7. **SHA-256 Linkability & Evidentiary Status:** The SHA-256 cryptographic hash (`audit_image_hash`) retained in `attendance_evidence` is a deterministic integrity fingerprint.   8. **Audit Trail Logging Without Image Bytes:** Deletion activity is recorded in `audit_logs` (capturing evidence ID, deletion timestamp, acting role `SYSTEM`, action `RETENTION_PURGE`, and outcome). Under no circumstances are actual selfie image bytes, base64 payloads, or thumbnails retained in audit records.
  9. **External Provider Contractual Alignment:** Any commercial biometric vendor engaged under DEC-03 must contractually support compatible zero-retention or deletion terms.
  10. **Statutory Legal Qualification:** Marked as a proposed policy subject to formal review and verification by qualified Indian legal counsel under the DPDP Act 2023, IT SPDI Rules 2011, and Telangana Shops and Establishments Act 1988 (LEG-04). Does not modify locked PRD v1.6.
