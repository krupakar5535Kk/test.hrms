## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) establishes the technical and functional requirements for the HRMS + Smart Attendance + Payroll SaaS platform. It translates the high-level business rules and acceptance criteria from PRD v1.9 into verifiable engineering specifications.

### 1.2 System Scope
The system automates the complete employee lifecycle, biometric and geofenced attendance verification, leave lifecycle management, precision working-hour accounting, and attendance-driven payroll generation. The initial deployment serves a single office with approximately 10 employees, but all architectural components are designed to be tenant-aware and multi-office capable.

### 1.3 Definitions and Abbreviations
- **PWA:** Progressive Web Application.
- **RLS:** PostgreSQL Row Level Security.
- **RTM:** Requirements Traceability Matrix.
- **Paise:** $1/100\text{th}$ of an Indian Rupee ($\text{INR}$). Monetary values are stored as 64-bit integers.
- **Standard Working Day:** 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Monday through Saturday).
- **Scheduled Duration:** 8.0 hours per scheduled working day.
- **Paid Lunch Interval:** 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM (Credited as 60 payable minutes on scheduled working days; **not deducted** from salary).
- **Sundays & Recognized Public Holidays:** Fully paid weekly offs and recognized holidays; automatically receive an 8.0-hour (480-minute) credit in the monthly payroll calculation.
- **Grace Period:** 10 minutes (Arrival after 10:10 AM is classified as `Late`; departure before 5:50 PM is classified as `Early Departure`). Both are descriptive tracking flags without independent monetary penalties.
- **Overtime / Extended Hours:** Work performed beyond 6:00 PM; credited only with Admin approval at a flat $1.0\times$ normal hourly rate.

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 User Classes and Roles
The system enforces strict role-based access control (RBAC):

1. **Super Admin (Role: `SUPER_ADMIN`):**
   - Holds overarching system governance.
   - Provisions, activates, and deactivates Admin and Employee accounts.
   - Defines organization-wide payroll policies, holiday calendars, and office geofence locations.
   - Performs final audit review and provides the mandatory approval (or rejection) for monthly payroll batches.
   - Exercises exclusive access to global audit trails, security logs, and salary revision histories.

2. **Admin (Role: `ADMIN`):**
   - Manages departmental rosters, designations, Sales Team field-attendance department eligibility, and employee records.
   - Monitors daily attendance feeds, reviews biometric/GPS verification failures, **reviews and approves/rejects Sales Team Field Attendance Requests** (inspecting selfie, biometric telemetry, GPS coordinates/accuracy, comment, and same-day attendance state), and processes manual missing punches or Workflow B timestamp corrections with mandatory justification notes.
   - Reviews and acts upon employee leave requests.
   - Compiles monthly attendance records, verifies adjustments, and initiates payroll batch generation for Super Admin approval.

3. **Employee (Role: `EMPLOYEE`):**
   - Accesses the responsive portal via mobile PWA or desktop web using organization code, mobile number, and password (DEC-13 & DEC-09 Approved).
   - Executes standard geofenced, biometric Check-In and Check-Out actions when attending the office (mandatory for all employees, including Sales Team employees at office).
   - **Sales Team Field Attendance Exception (Sales Team Employees Only):** Employees assigned to an Admin-designated Sales Team department may submit a separate **Field Attendance Request** (live selfie, face match, liveness, captured field GPS coordinates/accuracy, and mandatory comment) strictly when working at a distant customer/sales location and unable to attend the office. Normal (non-Sales) employees must neither see nor access this feature.
   - Monitors personal attendance history, daily logged hours, field request statuses, monthly accrued hours, real-time estimated earned salary, approved leave balances, and published payslips.
   - Submits leave applications and views supervisor review outcomes.
   - Strictly restricted from altering attendance data, self-approving field attendance, or inspecting peers' records.

### 2.2 System Operating Environment
- **Client Tier:** Responsive Web and PWA rendered via React 18+, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui. Accessible on modern mobile browsers (iOS Safari, Android Chrome) with camera and HTML5 Geolocation API support.
- **Application Tier:** Node.js 20+ LTS executing Express.js modular monolith with structured layered services (Controllers, Services, Repositories).
- **Data Tier:** Managed PostgreSQL 15+ hosted on Supabase, leveraging native Row Level Security (RLS), ACID transactions, and automated daily snapshots.
- **Object Storage:** Private Supabase Storage buckets storing biometric enrollment references and daily attendance snapshots, accessible exclusively via short-lived native Supabase Storage pre-signed URLs (15-minute expiration).

## 3. Mathematical & Algorithmic Formulations (Amended v1.7 Baseline)

### 3.1 Calendar-Day Monthly Salary Reference Model & Precision Architecture
The agreed monthly salary reference ($S_{\text{ref}}$, expressed in integer paise) is distributed across all calendar days of the specific payroll month ($D_{\text{month}} \in \{28, 29, 30, 31\}$):

$$\text{Daily Rate (Paise)} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}}}$$

$$\text{Hourly Rate (Paise)} = \frac{\text{Daily Rate}}{8} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 8}$$

$$\text{Minute Rate (Paise)} = \frac{\text{Hourly Rate}}{60} = \frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480}$$

*Precision & Rounding Architecture:*
- **Data-Model Distinction (Amounts vs. Derived Rates):**
  1. *Monetary Amounts:* Base monthly reference salary ($S_{\text{ref}}$), gross earned salary, allowances, deductions, approved adjustments, and net payable salary are whole-currency figures modeled and stored as exact 64-bit integer paise (`BIGINT`), constrained by check bounds (`CHECK >= 0` where applicable).
  2. *Derived Rates for Reporting & Display:* Daily rate ($\frac{S_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{month}}}$), hourly rate ($\frac{\text{Daily Rate}}{8}$), and minute rate ($\frac{\text{Hourly Rate}}{60}$) are derived values. Fractional paise frequently result (e.g. ₹20,000 / 30 yields 66,666.666667 paise/day, 8,333.333333 paise/hr, and 138.888889 paise/min). Storing derived rates as `NUMERIC(14,6)` provides fractional decimal precision for reporting and payslip display; however, `NUMERIC(14,6)` is fractional decimal representation, **not exact rational arithmetic**, and must **never** be used as the authoritative calculation source for accumulated earnings. Storing derived rates in integer `BIGINT` columns is strictly prohibited.
- **Correction of Rounding Invalidation:** The claim that a rounded six-decimal minute rate guarantees zero truncation loss is mathematically false. In any month where $S_{\text{ref}} / (D_{\text{month}} \times 480)$ produces a repeating non-terminating decimal (e.g. ₹20,000 in a 30-day month = $138.888888...$ paise/min), multiplying accumulated minutes by a rounded six-decimal minute rate introduces monetary drift ($14,400 \times 138.888889 = 2,000,001.6$ paise = ₹20,000.02). Daily rounding compounds this error up to 10 paise. Internal precision (`NUMERIC(14,6)` paise) is retained strictly for intermediate rate displays.
- **Authoritative Accumulated Earnings Calculation (Exact Rational Arithmetic):** To guarantee exact full-month reconciliation with zero truncation drift, payroll aggregation evaluates accumulated employee earnings directly from total approved payable minutes using exact rational numerator and denominator arithmetic before applying final monetary rounding:
  $$\text{segment\_earnings\_paise}_k = \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}, k} \times S_{\text{ref}, k, \text{paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480}$$
  Unrounded segment earnings are summed across DEC-15 effective-date segments with full rational precision, and the combined gross amount is rounded once to integer paise:
  $$\text{Gross Earned (Paise)} = \text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \sum_{k} \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}, k} \times S_{\text{ref}, k, \text{paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$$
- **Uniform Integer-Paise Amount Precision:** All final currency representations—including gross earned salary, allowances, adjustments, deductions, and net payable salary—are modeled and stored as 64-bit integer paise (`BIGINT`), ensuring strict mathematical consistency across the calculation pipeline.
- **Net Salary Formulation:**
  $$\text{Net Salary (Paise)} = \text{Gross Earned (Paise)} + \sum \text{Allowances (Paise)} + \sum \text{Adjustments (Paise)} - \sum \text{Deductions (Paise)}$$
- **Open Decision on Whole-Rupee Settlement Rounding (DEC-05: Net Salary Rounding Rule):** Rounding net salary to whole rupees for cash payout or bank transfer display is an open Product Owner decision (`DEC-05`). The mandatory calculation engine maintains exact integer paise unless the Product Owner explicitly confirms a separate settlement or display rounding rule.

### 3.2 Scheduled Working Day Payable Minutes (Paid Lunch & Absence Rules)
For any scheduled working day (Monday through Saturday):
1. **Approved Actual Working Minutes:**
   $$M_{\text{actual\_approved}} = T_{\text{out}} - T_{\text{in}} - M_{\text{unapproved\_post18}}$$
   *(Where actual working time is independently established, verified, and approved; unapproved time after 18:00 is excluded).*
2. **Absence & Paid Lunch Policy (UNASSIGNED-PO-ABSENCE-LUNCH — Proposed Engineering Default / Open Decision):**
   - *Confirmed Benchmarks:*
     - Full Standard Day: 7.0h approved actual work (420m) + 1.0h paid lunch (60m) = 8.0 payable hours (480m) = Daily Rate.
     - Partial Day Benchmark: 5.0h approved actual work (300m) + 1.0h paid lunch (60m) = 6.0 payable hours (360m).
   - *Absence Status:* No explicit Product Owner decision decree exists defining whether zero lunch credit is awarded on absence. The engineering design default sets $M_{\text{actual\_approved}} = 0$, $M_{\text{lunch\_credit}} = 0$, and $\text{Daily Payable Minutes} = 0$ for an employee who records 0 approved actual working minutes and has no approved paid leave (`Absent`). This default is cataloged under open decision `UNASSIGNED-PO-ABSENCE-LUNCH` pending formal Product Owner confirmation.
   - *Separation of Policy Domains:* Full absence (`UNASSIGNED-PO-ABSENCE-LUNCH`), partial-day attendance qualification (`UNASSIGNED-PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY`), leave pay treatment (`UNASSIGNED-PO-LEAVE-PAY: Leave Pay Treatment and Salary Model Reconciliation`), and weekend/holiday spans (`UNASSIGNED-PO-HOLIDAY-LEAVE`) are distinct policy domains and must not be conflated.
3. **Confirmed Benchmark Attendance:**
   - **Full Standard Day:** Employee completes approved 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (with normal 1-hour lunch break).
     $$M_{\text{actual\_approved}} = 420\text{ mins (7.0h)}, \quad M_{\text{lunch\_credit}} = 60\text{ mins (1.0h)}$$
     $$M_{\text{payable}} = 420 + 60 = 480\text{ mins (8.0h)}$$
     $$\text{Daily Earned Value} = 8.0 \times \text{Hourly Rate} = \text{Daily Rate}$$
   - **Confirmed Partial Day Benchmark:** Employee completes 5.0 hours approved actual work (300 mins) and takes lunch:
     $$M_{\text{actual\_approved}} = 300\text{ mins (5.0h)}, \quad M_{\text{lunch\_credit}} = 60\text{ mins (1.0h)}$$
     $$M_{\text{payable}} = 300 + 60 = 360\text{ mins (6.0h)}$$
   - **Extended Day with Approved Post-18:00 Work:** Employee completes 10:00 AM – 7:30 PM (9.5 elapsed hours). Admin approves 90 mins post-18:00:
     $$M_{\text{actual\_approved}} = 420 + 90 = 510\text{ mins (8.5h)}, \quad M_{\text{lunch\_credit}} = 60\text{ mins (1.0h)}$$
     $$M_{\text{payable}} = 510 + 60 = 570\text{ mins (9.5h)}$$
     *(Approved overtime paid at flat $1.0\times$ hourly rate).*
4. **Open Policy Decision on Partial Attendance (PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY):**
   The qualification criteria for crediting 60 minutes of paid lunch during partial attendance remain an open Product Owner decision:
   - *Case 1 (Pre-Lunch Only):* Employee works 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM (3.0 actual hours) and departs. Does employee qualify for paid lunch credit?
   - *Case 2 (Post-Lunch Only):* Employee arrives at 2:00 PM and works until 6:00 PM (4.0 actual hours). Does employee qualify for paid lunch credit?
   - *Case 3 (Span Across Lunch):* Employee works 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM (4.0 actual hours spanning the 1:00–2:00 PM lunch window). Does employee qualify for paid lunch credit?
   - *Case 4 (Partial-Day Leave):* Approved half-day leave overlapping the lunch window.
   - *Case 5 (Minimum Actual Hours Threshold):* Whether a minimum threshold of actual work (e.g. $\ge 4.0\text{ hours}$) is required before the 60-minute paid lunch credit applies.

### 3.3 Automatic Paid Credits for Sundays & Recognized Public Holidays
1. **Sundays (Paid Weekly Off):**
   - Automatically credited with **8.0 paid hours (480 minutes)** in the monthly calculation.
   - Office attendance is not required.
   - $\text{Payable Minutes} = 480$; $\text{Daily Earned Salary} = 8.0 \times \text{Hourly Rate} = \text{Daily Rate}$.
2. **Recognized Public Holidays (Paid Holidays):**
   - Recognized organization public holidays automatically receive an **8.0 paid hours (480-minute)** credit.
   - Office attendance is not required.
   - $\text{Payable Minutes} = 480$; $\text{Daily Earned Salary} = 8.0 \times \text{Hourly Rate} = \text{Daily Rate}$.
3. **Overlapping Sunday and Public Holiday Rule:**
   - When a recognized public holiday falls on a Sunday, the system credits **exactly 480 minutes (8.0 hours)**.
   - Dual credit ($960\text{ minutes}$) is strictly prohibited. Both calendar classifications are preserved in the calendar registry.

### 3.4 DEC-15 Approved Option A: Effective-Date Salary Proration
When an employee's monthly salary reference changes mid-month:
1. The month is partitioned into continuous date segments $k$, each governed by the reference salary active on those dates ($S_{\text{ref}, k}$).
2. Each segment's unrounded earnings are evaluated with full internal precision using that segment's approved payable minutes and the month's calendar-day divisor ($D_{\text{month}} \times 480$):
   $$\text{segment\_earnings\_paise}_k = \frac{M_{\text{payable}, k} \times S_{\text{ref}, k, \text{paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480}$$
3. The unrounded segment earnings are summed across all segments:
   $$\text{unrounded\_gross\_paise} = \sum_{k} \text{segment\_earnings\_paise}_k$$
4. Intermediate rounding of individual segments is **strictly prohibited** to eliminate cumulative multi-segment rounding drift.
5. The closing salary reference is never applied retrospectively to prior dates.

### 3.5 Gross and Net Salary Formulation & Full-Month Reconciliation Proof
For an employee across a monthly payroll cycle:
1. **Total Monthly Gross Earned Salary (Paise):**
   Evaluated by summing unrounded segment earnings and applying Half-Up rounding once to the aggregate:
   $$\text{Total Gross Earned (Paise)} = \text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \sum_{k} \frac{M_{\text{payable}, k} \times S_{\text{ref}, k, \text{paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$$
   *(For a period with no mid-month salary change, this simplifies to $\text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \frac{\sum M_{\text{payable}} \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right)$).*
2. **Net Payable Salary (Paise):**
   $$\text{Net Salary (Paise)} = \text{Total Gross Earned (Paise)} + \sum \text{Allowances (Paise)} + \sum \text{Adjustments (Paise)} - \sum \text{Deductions (Paise)}$$
   Net salary retains exact integer-paise precision (`BIGINT`).
3. **Mathematical Full-Month Reconciliation Proof:**
   If an employee completes full attendance on all $D_{\text{month}}$ days of a month (with zero mid-month salary changes, zero unpaid absences, zero deductions):
   $$\sum M_{\text{payable}} = D_{\text{month}} \times 480\text{ minutes}$$
   Substituting into the accumulated gross formula:
   $$\text{Total Gross Earned (Paise)} = \text{ROUND\_HALF\_UP}\left( \frac{(D_{\text{month}} \times 480) \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \right) = S_{\text{ref, paise}}$$
   Since $S_{\text{ref, paise}}$ is an integer, $\frac{(D_{\text{month}} \times 480) \times S_{\text{ref, paise}}}{D_{\text{month}} \times 480} \equiv S_{\text{ref, paise}}$ exactly with remainder zero.
   Exact reconciliation holds unconditionally for all calendar month lengths:
   - $D_{\text{month}} = 28$ (February non-leap): $13,440\text{ mins} \rightarrow \frac{13,440 \times S_{\text{ref}}}{13,440} = S_{\text{ref}}$.
   - $D_{\text{month}} = 29$ (February leap year): $13,920\text{ mins} \rightarrow \frac{13,920 \times S_{\text{ref}}}{13,920} = S_{\text{ref}}$.
   - $D_{\text{month}} = 30$ (Apr, Jun, Sep, Nov): $14,400\text{ mins} \rightarrow \frac{14,400 \times S_{\text{ref}}}{14,400} = S_{\text{ref}}$.
   - $D_{\text{month}} = 31$ (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Aug, Oct, Dec): $14,880\text{ mins} \rightarrow \frac{14,880 \times S_{\text{ref}}}{14,880} = S_{\text{ref}}$.

### 3.6 Superseded Historical DEC-01 Deduction Formulation (Preserved for Traceability)
*(Historical Note: Under original DEC-01 approved in v1.4, lunch was deducted using exact window clipping:*
$$M_{\text{lunch\_deducted}} = \max\left(0, \min(T_{\text{out}}, \text{14:00}) - \max(T_{\text{in}}, \text{13:00})\right)$$
*This deduction formula is **formally superseded** by the v1.9 Paid Lunch Policy. Lunch is no longer subtracted from payable time).*

### 3.7 Critical Unresolved Policies & Technical Feasibility
1. **Work on Sunday Extra Compensation (UNASSIGNED-PO-SUN-WORK OPEN):** Sunday automatically credits 480 minutes. Whether actual work on Sunday yields additional pay beyond the 480-minute credit remains unresolved.
2. **Work on Public Holiday Extra Compensation (UNASSIGNED-PO-HOL-WORK OPEN):** Public holiday automatically credits 480 minutes. Whether actual work on a holiday yields additional pay beyond the 480-minute credit remains unresolved.
3. **Leave Pay Treatment & Model Reconciliation (UNASSIGNED-PO-LEAVE-PAY OPEN):** Existing baseline treats CL/SL/PL/LWP as 0 payable hours and 0 salary. Under a calendar-day salary model, reconciling internal company leave rules with the paid monthly distribution remains unresolved. This is an internal organizational policy matter, not an established statutory entitlement. Kept strictly separate from leave quota deduction metric `TBD-06`.
4. **Partial Attendance Lunch Eligibility Criteria (UNASSIGNED-PO-LUNCH-ELIGIBILITY OPEN):** Unresolved qualification rules for crediting 60m paid lunch when an employee works partial hours (pre-lunch only, post-lunch only, across lunch, partial leave, minimum hours threshold).
5. **Absence Paid Lunch Policy (UNASSIGNED-PO-ABSENCE-LUNCH OPEN):** Formal confirmation of whether zero lunch credit is awarded to absent employees (0 actual minutes worked and no approved paid leave).
6. **Net Salary Rounding Rule (DEC-05 OPEN):** Confirming whether net payout is rounded to nearest whole rupee for cash payment settlement/display or maintained in exact integer paise.
7. **Actual Working-Time Measurement Technical Blocker (BLOCKER-ACTUAL-WORK-MEASUREMENT):** The single-punch model (Check-In / Check-Out) records elapsed attendance span. Productive working time is not currently measurable under baseline punches. Elapsed presence cannot be equated with productive work, nor can inactivity be assumed to be non-work. Illustrative alternatives (active task logging, desktop monitoring, supervisor timesheet approval) represent possible approaches requiring separate evaluation and explicit approval; no surveillance or monitoring method is selected or recommended as an approved architecture. Implementation is blocked pending architectural design.

## 4. Detailed Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Authentication & Account Management (SRS-FR-AUTH)
- **SRS-FR-AUTH-01 (RBAC Enforcement):** The system shall enforce three discrete user roles: `SUPER_ADMIN`, `ADMIN`, and `EMPLOYEE`. All endpoints must authenticate identity and verify permissions prior to execution.
- **SRS-FR-AUTH-02 (Identifier & Credentials — DEC-13, DEC-09, `PO-01` Scheme 3 & `PO-02` Aligned):** Employee login shall require strictly `Organization Code` + canonical `Mobile Number` + `Password` (`POST /api/v1/auth/login`), enforcing **approved `PO-02` (`2026-11-10`)** Phase 1 Indian-mobile-only validation scoped uniquely per tenant (`uq_users_org_phone`), while allowing the same canonical mobile number across different organizations. **Employees must NEVER be asked to enter an email address.** Only three Indian mobile input shapes (`9876543210`, `919876543210`, `+919876543210`, with common spaces and hyphens stripped before validation) with exact 10 national significant digits starting with `6`, `7`, `8`, or `9` are accepted and normalized **authoritatively on the backend** to canonical E.164 `+91XXXXXXXXXX` (`public.users.phone_number` and `account_provisioning_requests.phone_number`); international phone numbers, landlines, short codes, malformed numbers, and arbitrary prefixes (including leading `0` trunk prefixes) shall be rejected cleanly without truncation or guesswork, and failed login attempts must return generic errors without revealing organization or phone existence. Under approved **`PO-01` Scheme 3 (`2026-11-10`)**, the backend resolves the internal immutable synthetic email `<immutable_uuid>@auth.hrms.internal` (generated at provisioning, persisted in `public.users.synthetic_email`, and unchanged when mobile numbers change) solely to broker Supabase Auth; it must never be exposed in employee-facing UI, API responses, logs, error messages, or payslips ([`docs/09_AUTHENTICATION_BROKER_ARCHITECTURE.md`](file:///Users/mac/Desktop/HRMS/docs/09_AUTHENTICATION_BROKER_ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- **SRS-FR-AUTH-03 (Secure Employee Activation & Password Privacy — Approved Policy):** When an account is provisioned via Maker-Checker approval and provisioning worker completion (`account_provisioning_requests.status = 'COMPLETED'`), the system shall generate a cryptographically secure 256-bit opaque random activation token (stored strictly as `token_hash CHAR(64)` via `SHA-256` in proposed `account_activation_tokens`) rendered as a single-use activation URL with a client-only fragment (`/auth/activate#token=...`) and equivalent QR code. Plaintext tokens must never be logged, persisted in browser analytics, or exposed in telemetry/error reporting. The provisioning Admin shall share the activation link or QR code with the employee strictly through an approved out-of-band channel (never exposed in public employee directories or ordinary API lists). Upon opening the link or scanning the QR code, the frontend shall extract the token from the URL fragment, strip the fragment from the address bar, and submit it over HTTPS. The backend shall validate the token and dispatch an automated SMS OTP to the employee's registered Indian mobile number (`+91XXXXXXXXXX`, `PO-02`). The employee shall complete activation by submitting `Organization Code + Mobile Number + Activation Token + OTP + New Password` (`POST /api/v1/auth/activate`). The backend shall verify the OTP using approved `PO-03` cryptographic principles (CSPRNG, short TTL, keyed `HMAC-SHA256` digest storage, single-use, max attempts, cooldown/rate limits, anti-replay) and verify the 256-bit token hash. Cross-system completion shall enforce a **user-level operation lock** (`public.users.activation_lock_state` keyed by `auth_user_id`) without holding open PostgreSQL locks across GoTrue calls (`NONE -> IN_FLIGHT` once, blocking Admin reissue and overlapping writes across tokens; releasing ONLY on verified GoTrue `422 weak_password` pre-commit rejection; client timeout/cancellation explicitly non-conclusive), atomically sending password and `app_metadata.hrms_activation_op_id` to GoTrue and reconciling uncertain outcomes positively on exact marker match, or quarantining the user account (`QUARANTINED_MANUAL_RECOVERY`) for controlled manual `SUPER_ADMIN`/SRE recovery before reissue (as **fully automatic safe retry is not technically possible with GoTrue**). On successful verification, the system shall set `activated_at = NOW()`, set `phone_verified_at = NOW()` (establishing mobile possession), clear `must_change_password = FALSE`, burn the token (`status = 'CONSUMED'`, `used_at = NOW()`) and OTP challenge, leave already-completed `account_provisioning_requests` untouched, and append an immutable `audit_logs` record. Employees shall **NEVER** be asked for an email address (`PO-01` Scheme 3 `<immutable_uuid>@auth.hrms.internal` preserved). Administrators shall **NEVER** create, view, set, or know employees' permanent passwords. Activation tokens shall automatically invalidate on successful activation, 24h expiry, cancellation, uncertain-outcome quarantine, or Admin reissue (`POST /api/v1/admin/users/:userId/reissue-activation` restricted to same-tenant Admins when lock is `'NONE'`). Repeated, expired, invalid, or used attempts shall fail safely without leaking account or organization existence.
- **SRS-FR-AUTH-04 (Authentication Rate Limiting & Error Responses — `PO-04` Approved — `2026-11-10`):** The authentication gateway shall enforce approved `PO-04` protections across login endpoints: **`5` failed login attempts per account within a rolling `15-minute` window** tracked via atomic shared counters across backend instances (no per-process in-memory counters) keyed by a privacy-preserving `HMAC` digest of normalized org identity + canonical E.164 mobile (`+91XXXXXXXXXX`), plus separate **IP-based rate limiting** respecting verified `trust proxy` configuration (ignoring untrusted client-supplied forwarding headers). Account throttling shall be strictly temporary (never permanent account lockout due only to failed logins); unknown organizations and unknown phones shall receive identical throttling (`HTTP 429` without account-existence disclosure, including `Retry-After` only when a reliable retry timestamp exists) and identical credential rejection payloads (`HTTP 401 INVALID_CREDENTIALS` without disclosing lookup-stage failures). Successful logins reset the account failure counter without clearing IP limits; layered timing mitigation is preserved (without claiming exact GoTrue constant time); audit logs capture failed attempts and throttling events strictly without passwords, OTPs, raw tokens, JWTs, or secret material; specific IP numeric ceilings and temporary tuning values remain implementation config, and `PO-03` OTP rules remain untouched.
- **SRS-FR-AUTH-05 (Self-Service Mobile OTP Recovery — `PO-03` Approved — & Revocation):** In Phase 1, activated employees with a registered, verified Indian mobile number (`+91XXXXXXXXXX` normalized per `PO-02`; `phone_verified_at IS NOT NULL`) shall recover forgotten passwords self-service using `Organization Code + Mobile Number + OTP + New Password` via `/api/v1/auth/reset-password/request-otp` and `/api/v1/auth/reset-password/confirm` without entering or exposing any email address, `synthetic_email`, or Supabase Auth ID. The backend shall resolve org/user eligibility without disclosing whether an account exists (`202 Accepted` on initiation, uniform generic error on failure), generate CSPRNG OTPs, persist only keyed `HMAC-SHA256` digests (`public.password_reset_otps` using Express-only pepper; zero plaintext OTP or unkeyed SHA-256), enforce short expiry, single-use atomic consumption under `FOR UPDATE`, max verification attempts, resend cooldowns, rate limits, replay/concurrency protection, password updates via trusted backend GoTrue Admin API (`admin.updateUserById`, never native Supabase phone auth), immediate global session revocation (`revoked_sessions` + `tokens_valid_after_epoch` + GoTrue global `signOut`), and immutable `audit_logs` omitting OTPs and passwords. SMS gateway provider and DLT integration remain unresolved under `DEC-10`, and concrete OTP numeric tuning defaults remain subject to operational/`PO-04` sign-off. ### 4.3 Office Geofencing & Work Schedule (SRS-FR-OFF)
- **SRS-FR-OFF-01 (Office Geofence Definition — `PO-ATT-GEOFENCE-50M` Proposed):** Each office entity shall store name, street address, latitude, longitude, and configurable geofence radius in meters (`100m` legacy baseline; **proposed tightening to `50m` standard office radius pending formal Product Owner approval**).
- **SRS-FR-OFF-02 (Haversine Distance Validation — Mandatory Standard Attendance Gate):** The backend shall compute distance between the client's reported GPS coordinates and the office location using the Great Circle Haversine formula:
  $$d = 2R \arcsin\left(\sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta\phi}{2}\right) + \cos\phi_1\cos\phi_2\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta\lambda}{2}\right)}\right)$$
  Where $R = 6,371,000\text{ meters}$. For standard office attendance (mandatory for ALL employees, including Sales Team members when attending the office), if $d > \text{office.geofence\_radius\_meters}$ (`50m` proposed / `100m` legacy baseline), the normal punch attempt must be **rejected** (`422 GEOFENCE_VIOLATION`). Normal office attendance shall also be evaluated against anti-spoofing rules and require both geofence pass and biometric pass for automatic approval. For **Sales Team Field Attendance Requests (`SRS-FR-ATT-07`)**, Haversine distance $d$ is still calculated and stored truthfully for audit visibility, without rejecting outside-office coordinates.
- **SRS-FR-OFF-03 (GPS Accuracy Enforcement):** Standard attendance punches AND Sales Team Field Attendance Requests reporting a GPS horizontal accuracy circle exceeding 30 meters ($A_{\text{reported}} > 30\text{m}$) shall be rejected with an instruction for the employee to retry satellite acquisition (`422 GPS_ACCURACY_INSUFFICIENT`).

### 4.4 Smart Attendance & Verification (SRS-FR-ATT)
- **SRS-FR-ATT-01 (Atomic Punch Flow):** Attendance submission requires a simultaneous payload: Device Geolocation (Lat, Long, Accuracy), Live Camera Snapshot (Selfie JPEG), Device Timestamp, and Action Type (`CHECK_IN` or `CHECK_OUT`).
- **SRS-FR-ATT-02 (Single-Pair Daily Session Enforcement - DEC-02 Approved):**
  - **Maximum One Pair Boundary:** Each employee is restricted to a **maximum of one Check-In event and maximum of one Check-Out event per scheduled working day** ($\le 1$ check-in, $\le 1$ check-out), forming at most a single working interval.
  - **No Forced Punches:** Absence, leave, and incomplete attendance are normal, valid business states. The system shall never fabricate or force punch events merely to satisfy the single-pair attendance boundary.
  - **Check-In Validation:** A second Check-In attempt on the same calendar day shall be rejected with HTTP 409 Conflict (`ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`).
  - **Deterministic Check-Out Validation Order:** When processing a Check-Out request, the engine shall evaluate in strict sequential order:
    1. If a valid `CHECK_OUT` already exists for this day $\rightarrow$ reject immediately with HTTP 409 Conflict (`ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_COMPLETED`).
    2. Otherwise, if no valid `CHECK_IN` exists for this day $\rightarrow$ reject with HTTP 409 Conflict (`CHECK_OUT_WITHOUT_CHECK_IN`).
    3. Otherwise $\rightarrow$ proceed with normal check-out verification and registration.
  - Multiple attendance sessions or split shifts are strictly prohibited in Phase 1 and Phase 2.
  - Employees are strictly prohibited from independently modifying, replacing, or deleting attendance records.
- **SRS-FR-ATT-03 (Unpaired / Incomplete Attendance Handling & Exceptional Unpaired Check-Out Policy):**
  - **Rejection of Normal Employee Unpaired Check-Out:** Any attempt by an employee to submit a check-out via the client API without an active, verified check-in for the day shall be strictly rejected with HTTP 409 Conflict (`CHECK_OUT_WITHOUT_CHECK_IN`). No employee-facing workflow exists to create or submit an unpaired check-out.
  - **Origins of Unpaired Check-Out Records:** An unpaired check-out record can never be created through employee actions. It may only originate via: (1) exceptional administrative manual entry (e.g. logging departure during an ongoing investigation of check-in issues), (2) legacy system historical data import, or (3) database anomaly / integrity recovery.
  - **Enforcement & Zero Payable Minutes:** Any attendance day containing an unpaired check-in (missing check-out) or an unpaired check-out (missing check-in) shall be automatically flagged with `requires_admin_review = true`. Under no circumstances shall an unpaired punch record generate payable working minutes; payable minutes remain strictly zero ($0$) until an authorized Admin explicitly resolves, matches, or corrects the record.
  - **Administrative Scope:** This requirement acts as an internal data-integrity safeguard and shall not introduce any new employee-facing attendance workflow.
- **SRS-FR-ATT-04 (Admin Attendance Corrections & Dual-Workflow Data Model):** Only an authorized Admin (or Super Admin) may perform attendance corrections. The system shall strictly distinguish two correction workflows while preserving database constraint `UNIQUE(attendance_day_id, event_type)`:
  - **Workflow A (Missing Punch Creation):** Admin creates the missing manual attendance event in `attendance_events` with `is_manual_entry = true`. Requires the actual reported timestamp, mandatory reason, Admin identity, and server timestamp. All original audit evidence is preserved.
  - **Workflow B (Incorrect Existing Punch Correction):** When correcting an erroneous existing punch (e.g. wrong punch time or accidental early check-out), the system **shall not create a duplicate CHECK_IN or CHECK_OUT event** and **shall not overwrite the original raw `attendance_events` record**. Instead, the system logs the change in `attendance_corrections` (with `previous_state` and `updated_state` JSONB, Admin ID, and mandatory reason) and updates the effective timestamp (`first_check_in_at` or `last_check_out_at`) on `attendance_days`. Attendance calculations and payroll strictly consume the approved effective timestamp.
- **SRS-FR-ATT-05 (Idempotency Semantics & Replay Safety):** The attendance ingestion endpoint shall enforce strict idempotency semantics via the `idempotency_keys` table:
  - **Same Idempotency-Key + Identical Request Payload:** Returns the original stored HTTP response (e.g. HTTP 200/201) immediately from cache/database without re-executing business logic or creating duplicate records.
  - **Different Idempotency-Key + Duplicate Punch Action:** Rejected with HTTP 409 Conflict (`ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN` or `ATTENDANCE_ALREADY_COMPLETED`).
  - **Same Idempotency-Key + Different Request Payload:** Rejected with HTTP 422 Unprocessable Entity (`IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`).
  - This guarantees that network retries never generate duplicate attendance events or duplicate payable minutes.
- **SRS-FR-ATT-06 (Offline Sync Queue):** In the event of network disruption, the client PWA may store encrypted punch attempts locally. Synchronized punches received by the server must be marked `is_offline_sync: true`, treated as untrusted, and routed to `Verification Pending` for mandatory Admin review. Offline submissions are never auto-approved.
- **SRS-FR-ATT-07 (Sales Team Field Attendance Request Exception — `PO-ATT-FIELD-SALES` Proposed Pending PO Sign-Off):**
  - **Exclusive Sales Team Eligibility:** The UI shall render and the backend shall authorize Field Attendance Requests **exclusively** for active employees assigned to a department flagged `allow_field_attendance = TRUE`. Standard (non-Sales) employees must not see or access this workflow (`403 FIELD_ATTENDANCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`). When attending an office, Sales Team employees must use standard office attendance (`SRS-FR-OFF-02`).
  - **DEC-02 Single-Pair & Explicit Punch Type (`CHECK_IN` / `CHECK_OUT`):** Every field attendance request must carry an explicit `request_type` (`CHECK_IN` or `CHECK_OUT`), and a field-work day enforces the exact same DEC-02 limit of at most one `CHECK_IN` and one `CHECK_OUT` per employee per scheduled day. **Both** field `CHECK_IN` and field `CHECK_OUT` independently require their own live camera selfie, successful face match + liveness check, current field GPS coordinates + horizontal accuracy ($\le 30\text{m}$ mandatory; $> 30\text{m}$ rejected for retry) + timestamp, anti-spoofing checks, and mandatory explanatory comment ($\ge 10$ trimmed characters). Exempts ONLY the 50m office geofence check; actual field coordinates and computed office distance must be retained truthfully without falsifying `is_within_office_geofence`.
  - **Mandatory Review State, Conflict Prevention & Ordering:** Requests enter strictly `PENDING_REVIEW` (never auto-approved). Database constraints (`uq_field_req_active_emp_day_type`) and validation enforce duplicate prevention across active/approved field requests and verified `attendance_events`, as well as check-in-before-check-out ordering (`CHECK_OUT_WITHOUT_CHECK_IN`).
- **SRS-FR-ATT-08 (Admin Field Attendance Review, Biometric Gate & Standard Reconciliation):**
  - **Independent Per-Request Admin Review (`ADMIN` / `SUPER_ADMIN` Only):** Each field `CHECK_IN` and `CHECK_OUT` request is independently reviewed. The Admin queue displays employee identity & Sales Team eligibility, employee comment, live selfie (via 15-minute private pre-signed URL; zero public URLs) alongside biometric match/liveness status, field GPS coordinates & accuracy, submission timestamp, spoof flags, and existing `attendance_days` / `attendance_events` records for that date.
  - **Failed Biometric Prohibition:** Admin **cannot approve** any field attendance request where face matching OR liveness verification failed (`is_liveness_verified = FALSE OR biometric_verified = FALSE` rejected with `422 BIOMETRIC_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` and DB `CHECK` constraint). Failed-biometric cases route through `Verification Pending` (`0` payable credit) and require successful identity verification via an authorized **Admin-supervised process** before attendance can be credited.
  - **Decisions & Immutability:** Admin selects **`APPROVE`** or **`REJECT`**, recording `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`, and mandatory `rejection_reason` ($\ge 10$ chars) when rejected, plus immutable `audit_logs` capture. Pending requests create zero payable credit; rejected requests create zero events and **do not automatically force `ABSENT`** (existing daily attendance resolution continues to govern).
  - **Single-Event Creation & Payroll Integrity:** Approving a request (blocked if target date's `payroll_periods.is_closed = TRUE` or if no prior `CHECK_IN` exists for a `CHECK_OUT`) reconciles **exactly one** corresponding immutable `attendance_events` row (`is_field_attendance = TRUE`, factual `is_within_office_geofence = FALSE`, preserving original field telemetry and submission `server_timestamp`) without overwriting raw events or creating duplicate credit. All approved events execute through the **single standard `attendance_days` and payroll calculation engine** (zero separate field attendance calculation or payroll mechanism).

### 4.5 Biometric Verification & Adapter Interface (SRS-FR-BIO)
- **SRS-FR-BIO-01 (Selfie Capture & Liveness):** The client interface shall prompt for a live camera feed (rejecting file picker uploads) and enforce frontend liveness checks (e.g., eye blink or head orientation challenge). This applies equally to standard office punches and Sales Team field attendance requests.
- **SRS-FR-BIO-02 (Biometric Enrollment):** An employee's initial biometric enrollment reference photo must be captured under Admin supervision and marked active only upon explicit Admin approval.
- **SRS-FR-BIO-03 (Verification Attempt Limiting & `Verification Pending` Rule):** An employee is granted up to three consecutive automated verification attempts for both standard attendance and field attendance requests. If face verification fails on the third attempt (or liveness fails), the submission enters **`Verification Pending`** (`verification_status = 'PENDING_REVIEW'` / `biometric_verified = FALSE`), retains failed probe images in private audit storage, and notifies the Admin. **`Verification Pending` is never attendance approval and creates zero payable attendance credit.** On field attendance requests specifically, an Admin **cannot** approve a request with failed liveness or face matching; successful identity verification through an authorized Admin-supervised process (e.g., Admin-supervised live re-verification / re-enrollment under `SRS-FR-BIO-02`) is required before attendance can be credited.
- **SRS-FR-BIO-04 (Daily Attendance Selfie 30-Day Retention & Deletion Policy — DEC-14 Proposed):**
  - **Strict 30-Day Maximum Ceiling:** Daily attendance selfies (covering both standard attendance and Sales Team field attendance requests in private bucket `attendance-evidence`) shall be retained for a maximum permitted period of 30 calendar days (DEC-14 Proposed, subject to Indian legal counsel review). Individual organizations may configure a shorter retention window (e.g., 7, 14, or 21 days via `organizations.selfie_retention_days`), but the database strictly enforces `CHECK (selfie_retention_days BETWEEN 1 AND 30)`, prohibiting any configuration exceeding 30 calendar days.
  - **Retention-Clock Starting Point (Unresolved Policy/Architecture Clarification):** The precise starting reference for the 30-day retention countdown has not been formally approved. An architectural distinction exists between device capture time (`ae.captured_at` from `device_timestamp`, which is client-reported and subject to device clock skew/manipulation) and trusted server receipt time (`ae.created_at`, which is server-monotonic). The authoritative starting point is recorded as an **unresolved policy and architectural clarification** under DEC-14, while strictly preserving the approved 30-day maximum ceiling.
  - **Comprehensive Multi-Tier Deletion Coverage:** Upon expiration of the configured retention period ($\le 30$ days), an automated background worker executes deletion across all four storage and processing layers:
    1. *Primary Object Storage:* The original selfie image file is permanently deleted from the private Supabase Storage bucket `attendance-evidence`.
    2. *Temporary Processing Copies & Buffers:* In-memory image buffers, base64 payload strings, and temporary file paths on application server local storage (`/tmp`) must be completely cleared immediately upon initial upload processing (0-second persistence post-upload).
    3. *Applicable Backup Expiry Horizon:* Secondary database backups, WAL archives, and object storage snapshots expire naturally per their respective lifecycle schedules (e.g., 7-day to 30-day point-in-time recovery horizon). The system ensures that disaster recovery restore operations do not resurrect previously purged images.
    4. *Third-Party Biometric Provider Compliance:* Where an external commercial biometric API is utilized (DEC-03), verification requests must be processed under contractual zero-retention SLAs (RAM-only probe processing without caching or model training), or an explicit deletion API command must be dispatched using `attendance_evidence.provider_transaction_id`.
  - **Legal Hold Suspension:** If an attendance dispute, wage claim, formal investigation, disciplinary inquiry, or legal hold exists (`is_legal_hold = TRUE`), automated deletion across all tiers is strictly suspended for the relevant evidence until the legal hold is formally released by an authorized Admin with a recorded justification.
  - **Post-Release Processing:** Following the formal administrative release of a legal hold (`is_legal_hold = FALSE`), the standard retention evaluation immediately applies, and records whose retention age exceeds the organization's retention ceiling proceed to automated purge.
  - **SHA-256 Linkability & Evidentiary Classification:** The SHA-256 hash (`attendance_evidence.audit_image_hash`) preserved post-purge is a deterministic cryptographic fingerprint intended solely for data integrity verification and tamper detection. Under data protection legislation (including DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR), cryptographic hashes of biometric data remain pseudonymous, potentially linkable personal data, and must NOT be treated as anonymous information or as a legal/visual substitute for original photographic evidence in dispute or judicial proceedings.
  - **Differentiated Statutory Retention Schedules:** Biometric selfie deletion is strictly decoupled from statutory business records. The system shall NOT apply a universal retention duration across all record types. Distinct retention categories and their statutory bases are defined as follows (with exact durations marked for Indian legal counsel verification):
    1. *Daily Attendance Selfies (Verification Probes):* Maximum 30 calendar days (configurable 1–30 days; purpose and storage limitation under DPDP Act 2023).
    2. *Employment & Attendance Registers (Dates, Punch Timestamps, Verification Status, Payable Minutes):* Mandated retention for at least 3 years from the date of last entry pursuant to Section 62 of the Telangana Shops and Establishments Act, 1988 (Rules 1990, Form XXII Register of Employment). *[Pending Indian Legal Counsel Verification]*.
    3. *Wage & Payroll Registers (Wages paid, deductions, net salary, overtime, disbursement ledgers):* Mandated retention for at least 3 years under Section 13A of Payment of Wages Act, 1936 and Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (with standard corporate practice recommending 5–8 years). *[Pending Indian Legal Counsel Verification]*.
    4. *Taxation, TDS & Social Security Ledgers (Form 16/16A TDS, PF/ESI deductions):* 6 to 7 years pursuant to Section 44AA / Section 149 of the Income Tax Act, 1961; 5 years under Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952 and ESI Act, 1948. *[Pending Indian Legal Counsel Verification]*.
    5. *Biometric Telemetry & Enrollment Vectors:* Retained strictly during active employment; purged upon employee termination/exit under DPDP Act 2023 §8(7) purpose cessation. *[Pending Indian Legal Counsel Verification]*.
    6. *Audit Logs & Administrative Mutations:* Maintained append-only for at least 3 years to support labor dispute and forensic defense. *[Pending Indian Legal Counsel Verification]*.
  - **Audit Logging Without Image Bytes:** Deletion outcomes must be appended to `audit_logs` (capturing evidence ID, deletion timestamp, acting role `SYSTEM`, action `RETENTION_PURGE`, and outcome status `SUCCESS`). The audit log strictly records operational metadata without storing original image bytes, base64 payloads, or thumbnails.
  - **Statutory Qualification & Legal Disclaimer:** This policy is a proposed product-owner baseline subject to formal review and verification by qualified Indian legal counsel under the DPDP Act 2023, IT SPDI Rules 2011, and relevant labor enactments (LEG-04).

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

### 5.1 Security Requirements (NFR-SEC)
- **NFR-SEC-01 (Multi-Tenant Isolation & Trusted Context Establishment):** Untrusted clients (browsers, mobile PWAs) shall have no direct network or database access to PostgreSQL or PostgREST. All client requests terminate at the Node.js/Express API. The backend authentication middleware validates the caller's JWT/session, resolves the verified `user_id` and active `organization_id`, and completely discards any client-supplied tenant identifier. Context is established transaction-locally (`set_config('app.current_org_id', :trusted_org_id, true)`) under the dedicated runtime role `hrms_app_user` configured with `NOBYPASSRLS` and `NOSUPERUSER`. Automated multi-tenant isolation tests (`SUITE-TENANT-ISOLATION`, TC-TEN-01 to TC-TEN-12) must run in CI/CD on every pull request, verifying zero cross-tenant leakage across employees, attendance, payroll, documents, reports, direct APIs, payload manipulations, and database queries.
- **NFR-SEC-02 (Biometric Storage Isolation, 30-Day Retention Ceiling & Comprehensive Deletion Coverage):** Daily attendance selfies (both standard attendance and Sales Team field attendance requests) shall be retained for a maximum permitted period of 30 calendar days (DEC-14 Proposed, subject to Indian legal counsel review; configurable per organization with a strict database-enforced ceiling: `CHECK (selfie_retention_days BETWEEN 1 AND 30)`; the precise retention-clock starting point between device capture timestamp and trusted server receipt timestamp is an unresolved policy and architectural clarification). Public image URLs are strictly prohibited. Upon expiration, an automated background worker executes comprehensive deletion covering: (1) primary object storage in private Supabase Storage bucket `attendance-evidence`; (2) temporary processing buffers and local `/tmp` files on application servers (0-second persistence post-upload); (3) applicable backup expiry horizons (ensuring restored backups do not resurrect purged images); and (4) third-party biometric provider storage via contractual zero-retention SLAs or explicit deletion API calls. Deletion is automatically suspended if `is_legal_hold = TRUE` exists for the subject. Deletion activity is logged in `audit_logs` strictly without image bytes. Audit failure images, field request selfies, and enrollment reference photos must reside in private buckets, accessible exclusively to authorized Admins via ephemeral pre-signed URLs with a time-to-live (TTL) not exceeding 15 minutes.
- **NFR-SEC-03 (Complete Append-Only Immutable Audit Trail):** All administrative actions and system mutations—including employee records, attendance corrections, leave approvals, salary revisions, payroll preparation, dual-key approvals/reversals, payment status updates, and automated biometric purge executions—must append a comprehensive audit record to `audit_logs` capturing `organization_id`, `actor_user_id`, `actor_role`, `action`, `target_table`, `target_id`, `payload_before`, `payload_after`, `reason`, `ip_address`, `user_agent`, and `created_at`. For biometric purge executions, deletion activity is recorded without retaining the actual selfie bytes. Immutability is enforced through defense-in-depth: (1) `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE ON audit_logs FROM hrms_app_user, PUBLIC;` (halting application mutation queries with `SQLSTATE 42501`); (2) statement-level trigger `trg_audit_logs_no_mutation` raising `insufficient_privilege` as a secondary defense; and (3) table ownership restricted to the migration role (`supabase_admin`), ensuring application Super Admins (application-layer roles) have zero DB mutation capability. Verified via `SUITE-AUDIT` (`TC-AUD-01` to `TC-AUD-04`).
- **NFR-SEC-04 (Biometric Privacy vs. Differentiated Statutory Business Records & Legal Holds):** The system shall strictly separate biometric data deletion (DEC-14 30-day selfie lifecycle; enrollment photo purge on exit) from statutory business records. The system does not assume a single universal retention period across all data types. Distinct retention schedules must be maintained: (1) Daily selfies: maximum 30 calendar days; (2) Attendance & employment registers: 3 years under Telangana Shops and Establishments Act, 1988 §62 (Form XXII) [pending legal verification]; (3) Wage and payroll registers: 3 years under Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (5–8 years recommended practice) [pending legal verification]; (4) Tax and social security ledgers: 6–7 years under Income Tax Act, 1961 §44AA and 5 years under EPF/ESI Acts [pending legal verification]; (5) Biometric vectors/enrollments: active employment only; purged upon exit [pending legal verification]; (6) Audit logs: at least 3 years [pending legal verification]. SHA-256 hashes (`audit_image_hash`) retained post-purge are pseudonymous, potentially linkable personal data, and must NOT be treated as anonymous information or a substitute for original evidence. Deleting a selfie nullifies storage paths and records `retention_status = 'PURGED'` without altering attendance or payroll records. If an employee or record is marked `is_legal_hold = TRUE` (due to active litigation, wage dispute, or disciplinary inquiry), all automated retention purges and right-to-erasure workflows are strictly suspended until an audited administrative release.

### 5.2 Reliability, Backup & Disaster Recovery (NFR-REL)
- **NFR-REL-01 (Automated Backup Cadence & Plan Dependencies):** Database backups shall be automated via daily base snapshots (30-day retention) and weekly cold archives exported to independent secondary cloud storage (90-day operational / 1-year compliance retention). Continuous Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) for Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR) requires subscribing to the Supabase Pro Plan with the optional PITR Add-on (7-day or 30-day retention). Without the PITR add-on, standard Pro provides daily snapshots with an operational recovery window of up to 24 hours.
- **NFR-REL-02 (Recovery Design Targets vs. Verified Capabilities):** The system establishes an Engineering Design Target of Recovery Point Objective (RPO) $\le 1\text{ hour}$ (with active WAL PITR add-on, actual transaction loss in standard database failure is $\le 5\text{ minutes}$) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO) $\le 4\text{ hours}$. These metrics represent design targets and shall not be treated as certified SLAs until validated through staging/production benchmark restore tests.
- **NFR-REL-03 (Periodic Sandbox Restore Rehearsals):** Monthly automated CI/CD jobs shall restore production backup snapshots into an isolated sandbox environment, executing automated integrity scripts validating table counts (25 tables), relational constraints, organization employee counters, and financial checksums (`SUITE-DR`, TC-DR-01 to TC-DR-03).

### 5.3 Performance & Scalability (NFR-PERF)
- **NFR-PERF-01:** Attendance check-in API processing time (excluding external 3rd-party facial recognition latency) shall complete within $\le 300\text{ ms}$ at $95^{\text{th}}$ percentile.
- **NFR-PERF-02:** Monthly payroll generation for a 50-employee workforce shall compute and commit within $\le 2.0\text{ seconds}$.
- **NFR-PERF-03:** All tabular reporting queries must be indexed to maintain sub-second response times up to 100,000 attendance event rows.

### 5.4 Data Integrity & Precision (NFR-INT)
- **NFR-INT-01 (Monetary Amounts vs. Exact Rational Arithmetic):** Monetary calculations must never use binary floating-point numbers. Monetary amounts are stored as exact 64-bit integer paise (`BIGINT`). Derived rates may be stored as `NUMERIC(14,6)` for fractional reporting; however, `NUMERIC(14,6)` is fractional decimal precision, not exact rational arithmetic, and must not be used as the authoritative calculation source. Authoritative salary compilation must evaluate exact rational numerator/denominator arithmetic: $\text{segment\_earnings\_paise} = \frac{\text{payable\_minutes} \times \text{salary\_reference\_paise}}{\text{calendar\_days} \times 480}$, summed across DEC-15 segments and rounded once to integer paise.
- **NFR-INT-02:** Database constraints must enforce unique active check-in and check-out constraints per employee per calendar date (`UNIQUE(attendance_day_id, event_type)` under DEC-02) and prevent duplicate active or approved Sales Team field attendance requests per `(organization_id, employee_id, attendance_date, request_type)` (`uq_field_req_active_emp_day_type`, proposed `PO-ATT-FIELD-SALES`).
- **NFR-INT-03 (Calendar-Day Referential Integrity & Tenant Safety Guarantee):** Because standard SQL `CHECK` constraints cannot evaluate cross-table column values, and to preserve financial ledger immutability through intentional controlled denormalization (Financial Snapshot Pattern) while eliminating update/insertion anomalies, the database schema enforces referential integrity and tenant boundaries across the calculation hierarchy via chained declarative composite foreign keys: (1) `payroll_periods` enforces `CHECK (calendar_days = (end_date - start_date + 1))` and exports candidate superkey `UNIQUE (organization_id, id, calendar_days)`; (2) `payroll_runs` inherits `organization_id` and enforces composite foreign key `FOREIGN KEY (organization_id, payroll_period_id, calendar_days) REFERENCES payroll_periods(organization_id, id, calendar_days) ON DELETE RESTRICT` and exports candidate superkey `UNIQUE (organization_id, id, calendar_days)`; (3) `payroll_items` inherits `organization_id` and enforces composite foreign key `FOREIGN KEY (organization_id, payroll_run_id, calendar_days) REFERENCES payroll_runs(organization_id, id, calendar_days) ON DELETE RESTRICT`. In a single atomic tuple constraint, this physically eliminates both denominator divergence and cross-tenant reference leakage.
